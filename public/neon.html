Examination rules

  • Your exam is timed. The timer starts when you enter and continues if you refresh the page.
  • Answers save automatically. Wait for the saved message after choosing an answer.
  • Navigation rules vary. Your teacher may lock parts or set separate part timers.
  • Stay on the exam tab. Leaving it may be reported to your teacher when the connection is available.
  • No copying or pasting. Copy, cut, and paste are blocked and may be reported during the exam.
  • One device, one tab. A second opening may be detected and recorded. Refresh in the original tab to resume.
  • Scores are hidden until released. Use your exam code and student ID to check later.
