Examination rules

  • Timed, one attempt. The timer starts the moment you begin and is tracked by the server — refreshing the page does not add time.
  • Stay on this tab. Switching tabs or windows is recorded automatically.
  • No copying or pasting. Copy, cut, and paste are blocked and logged.
  • One device, one tab. Opening the exam a second time will be detected.
  • Questions are shuffled. Your paper is in a different order from everyone else's.
  • You can go back. Move between parts freely and change an answer any time before you submit.
  • Your answers are saved automatically after every response.
  • Scores are not shown on screen. Your teacher will release the results.
